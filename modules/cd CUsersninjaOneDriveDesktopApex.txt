## 12. METRICS — 6 КӨРСЕТКІШ

### 12.1. Формулалар

| № | Метрика | Формула |
|---|---------|---------|
| 1 | **Expectancy** | (WR × Avg_Win) - (LR × Avg_Loss) |
| 2 | **Profit Factor** | Gross_Profit / Gross_Loss |
| 3 | **Max Drawdown** | (Peak - Trough) / Peak × 100 |
| 4 | **Net Profit** | After commission |
| 5 | **Sharpe Ratio** | (Return - RF) / StdDev |
| 6 | **Recovery Factor** | Net_Profit / Max_DD |

### 12.2. Нормативтер

| Метрика | Жақсы | Орташа | Нашар |
|---------|-------|--------|-------|
| Win Rate | > 70% | 55-70% | < 55% |
| Profit Factor | > 2.0 | 1.3-2.0 | < 1.3 |
